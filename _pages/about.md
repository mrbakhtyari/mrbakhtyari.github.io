I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the **Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM)**, advised by Prof. [Vladimir Makarenkov](http://www.info2.uqam.ca/~makarenkov_v/site_web/index_en.html). My research lives at the intersection of **machine learning, deep learning, and bioinformatics** — I build models that turn noisy, high-dimensional data, from EEG signals to biological networks, into reliable predictions.

I enjoy taking an idea from a messy dataset all the way to a model that actually works, and I am currently looking for **research and ML engineering internships** where I can build that kind of impact at scale.

## Research Interests

- Graph Neural Networks &amp; Network Science
- Bioinformatics &amp; Computational Biology
