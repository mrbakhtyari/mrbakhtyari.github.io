Mohammadreza Bakhtyari

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM), advised by Vladimir Makarenkov. I develop learning and inference methods that generalize across datasets and tasks, with a focus on amortized and probabilistic inference, meta-learning, and structured neural architectures. My current work studies how synthetic priors and permutation-aware models transfer to real-world tabular data.

My doctoral research is supported by the FRQNT / PBEEE scholarship.

Research interests

  • Amortized & probabilistic inference
  • Meta-learning & in-context learning
  • Structured & permutation-aware architectures
  • Generalization & transfer
