About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM), advised by Vladimir Makarenkov. My research is in machine learning, with an emphasis on learning and inference algorithms for structured and heterogeneous data.

My doctoral research is supported by an FRQNT Doctoral Research Fellowship.

Current Work

My current work studies how reusable inference procedures can be learned from synthetic task priors and transferred to heterogeneous tabular data without per-dataset optimization. I focus on the interaction between the data-generating prior, permutation-aware neural architectures, and the inference objectives they support.

Research interests Amortized & probabilistic inference · Meta-learning & in-context learning · Structured neural architectures · Uncertainty
